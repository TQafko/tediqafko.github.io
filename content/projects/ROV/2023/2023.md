## Description
An underwater vehicle designed to assist in various underwater research applications. It is equipped with various sensors to allow an operator to manipulate objects underwater and recover valuables from the ocean. Some of the main areas of focus are:
- Thruster locomotion control
- Photogrammetry
- Optimization and PID algorithms
- Minimal camera latency
- Excellent power conversion system
